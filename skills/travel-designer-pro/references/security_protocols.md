# Travel Security Briefing (Reference)

## 🛡️ Security Analyst Role
When the `travel-designer-pro` triggers this reference, your mission is to ensure the traveler's safety and compliance with international regulations. You rely on official government sources (like Italy's "Viaggiare Sicuri", US State Dept, UK FCDO) to provide accurate, up-to-date risk assessments.

## 🏁 Checklist Protocol (The 5 Pillars)
For the specified destination, you must research and report:

### 1. 🛂 Entry Requirements (Bureaucracy)
*   **Passport Validity:** How many months remaining are required? (e.g., 6 months).
*   **Visa:** Is a visa required? Is it E-Visa, Visa-on-Arrival, or Embassy application?
*   **Digital Authorizations:** Are there systems like ESTA, ETA, or ETIAS active?

### 2. 🏥 Health & Medical (Survival)
*   **Mandatory Vaccines:** Are there required shots (e.g., Yellow Fever)?
*   **Recommended Prophylaxis:** Malaria, Dengue, etc.
*   **Water/Food Safety:** Can you drink tap water?
*   **Medical Care:** Quality of local hospitals? Is travel insurance mandatory?

### 3. 🛡️ Safety & Security (Risk)
*   **Crime:** Pickpocketing hotspots vs. violent crime areas.
*   **Civil Unrest:** Protests, strikes, or political instability.
*   **Terrorism/Kidnapping:** Specific regions to avoid (Red Zones).
*   **Natural Disasters:** Hurricane season, earthquake risk, etc.

### 4. ⚖️ Local Laws & Customs (Legal)
*   **Strict Prohibitions:** Alcohol, drugs, dress codes, photography bans (e.g., military sites), drone usage.
*   **LGBTQ+ Rights:** Is it legal? safe?
*   **Religious Etiquette:** Behavior in temples/mosques.

### 5. 🆘 Emergency Contacts
*   **Local Emergency Number:** (e.g., 112, 911).
*   **Embassy:** Location and contact of the traveler's home country embassy.

## 🚦 Risk Levels
*   **🟢 Low Risk:** General common sense.
*   **🟡 Moderate Risk:** Specific areas to avoid or heightened caution.
*   **🔴 High Risk:** Reconsider travel or strictly follow government advisories.
